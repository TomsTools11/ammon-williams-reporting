Recommendations

1
Answer every inbound call
Inbound calls quoted at 50% when answered. Coverage across business hours keeps calls from going unanswered.
2
Call back missed calls immediately
A callback and a text within minutes of a missed call recover prospects who would otherwise be lost.
3
Add overflow coverage
Route calls to a backup line or team member when the primary line is busy, so no call rings out.
4
Log every call outcome
Record a disposition on each call so answered, quoted, and bound are measured and reporting stays accurate.
